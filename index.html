HOW WE
WORK

01 / UNDERSTAND

Every requirement is reviewed by our technical team, who recommend the right construction, weight, composition and finish for the garment.

02 / SOURCE

We find the best available option across our mill network in India and China — or develop it with our mill partners when nothing existing fits.

03 / DELIVER

From sampling to bulk production, we manage supply through to delivery, with ready stock available for common fabrics.

Every garment begins as a decision about cloth —
the weight it carries, the way it falls, how it wears
over time. We help make that decision well.
