SHIRTING DENIM

Lightweight denim with a softer handle and better drape, used for shirts, overshirts and lighter bottoms.

HEAVY DENIM

Bottomweight denim with structure and durability, used for jeans, cargos, jackets and workwear.

STRETCH

Two constructions.

Every quality in our denim range is offered in both constructions, and the choice shapes the garment more than almost any other decision. Stretch determines how a piece moves, how it holds its shape through the day, and how it ages over years of wear. Whichever direction a collection takes, the same weights and compositions are available either way.

Available across both weight classes, 4 to 14 oz, and every composition we supply.

01

LYCRA

Elastane blended into the weave for comfort stretch and recovery. The standard for modern fits — slim, tapered and anything worn close to the body. Holds its shape through the day and across repeated washes.

02

NON - LYCRA

Rigid denim woven from cotton alone, with no elastane. The traditional construction, and the one that fades and creases with wear, taking the shape of whoever wears it. Structured, firmer handle, and the character denim is known for.
