THE FAMILY

From fabric to finished garment. Indigo Multifab is the
sister company of Vintage Blue Jeanswear, a
contemporary Indian menswear brand established in
2006. Together, the two businesses span the full
journey of a garment — from sourcing the cloth to
designing, manufacturing and bringing it to market.
