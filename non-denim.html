STRETCH

Two constructions.

Stretch and non-stretch constructions offer different qualities across our Fabric range. Elastane blends provide added flexibility and recovery where needed, while non-stretch fabrics preserve their natural structure, drape and surface character. The choice influences how a garment moves, feels and performs.

Available across selected cotton, viscose and other woven qualities, with compositions and specifications tailored to the intended application.

01

STRETCHABLE

Cotton, viscose and selected woven fabrics blended with elastane to introduce stretch, flexibility and recovery. Suitable for applications where ease of movement and comfort are important, depending on the fabric construction and intended use.

02

NON-STRETCHABLE

Woven fabrics without added elastane, retaining their intended structure, drape and surface character. Ideal for applications that prioritise fabric stability and the natural qualities of linen, cotton, corduroy, jacquard, dobby and other woven constructions.
